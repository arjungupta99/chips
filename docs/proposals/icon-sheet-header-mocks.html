The head of the icon sheet

You said it reads busy. It is: four bands of chrome stand between opening the sheet and seeing a single mark, and two of them say the same thing twice. Three ways to cut it, drawn at phone width.

each dashed line is one band of chrome before the grid.

What is there now, and what is wrong with it

  • “Use the default” sits where Cancel goes. It is three words long, it is the least-used thing on the sheet, and it is in the most valuable corner.
  • “Icon” is a title that says nothing. You arrived by tapping Change icon…; the sheet does not need to tell you what it is.
  • The preview band repeats the row behind it. The name and the snippet are already on screen.

My call

A. It cuts the two bands that carry no information and keeps the two that do — the colours and the families. The head becomes the thing you are changing: the face, at the size it will be, next to the room's name, with Done where Done belongs. Use the default stops being a word in a corner and becomes the first cell of the grid, which is where you would look for it and where it reads as “no mark” rather than as an instruction.

C is there to show how far this goes if the family chips come out too. I would not: eighty-four marks in one scroll with only sticky headings is a lot of thumb, and the chips are the one piece of chrome earning its keep.
