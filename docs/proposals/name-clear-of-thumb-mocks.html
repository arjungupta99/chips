The same in all of them

  • The face under the finger still grows, and the field's words still say "Message" and the name. The field is under the thumb too, so neither can be the only place the name shows.
  • Letting go on a face picks that agent alone. Letting go off the column changes nothing. A tap still opens the list, with its circles for several.
  • Each face the finger reaches gives a light haptic tick, as now.
  • The thumb drawn here is 112 points wide, about 20 mm on an iPhone 17, its pad 30 points from its tip. Your own may be wider or narrower: try it on your phone.
