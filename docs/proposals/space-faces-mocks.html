Space faces, redrawn, and carried into the chat

Today's 84 marks are thin white outlines on flat colour. That is the same look as most off-the-shelf icon sets, so a room's face does not look like it belongs to Chips. At 28 points the lines also get very thin. That is the size the chat would draw them at, and the chat does not draw them at all today: the routing row shows initials. Below are four ways to redraw them, and the routing rows with the face you picked.

Every face on this page is one of your own rooms. The six marks you have picked (the live space_icons rows) are drawn on the grounds you picked them on. The switcher at the top redraws every phone.

★

Chosen, 22 September: C · Folded, with the library doubled to 168. Each mark is drawn as folded paper, lit from the upper left. The lit side is white, the shaded side is tinted with the ground, and details are cut through to the ground. It uses six fixed paints and one light direction, so all 168 marks look like one set, and it holds up at 28 points. It is also the most work of the four: every mark gets redrawn. B is the same drawings without the tints, so choosing C gets you B's drawings too. D adds a Shapes family on top of any option.

D · Shapes: a family with no pictures

Twelve compositions made from the same six paints and cropped by the tile. Some rooms have no obvious picture, like Value Strategy or Board Drill, and a rocket is a guess. A composition just tells two rooms apart, and it looks good while doing it. They could be a ninth family in the sheet. What each costs

Nothing on the wire changes in any option. The server stores a mark's name and a ground's index, so your six saved faces redraw in the new style without anyone picking again.
