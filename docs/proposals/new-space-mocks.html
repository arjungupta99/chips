A new space, from a + by the tab bar

A flat gold + sits beside the tab bar. Tap it and it stretches into a small window, Create new space, with a field above the keyboard. Say what the space is for and send. The window fills the screen and becomes the room, and your words become its first message. The room says New space until the harness names it.

This is a running mock, not the app. It plays on its own until you touch the phone. After that you can tap the +, type on your own keyboard or the drawn one, and press Enter. Turn on Slow motion to judge the movement.

The + : pick one

All four open the same window and move into the room the same way. The difference is how the button looks and how much of it is the system's own. None of them has shading or a highlight. The first version did, and it looked like a 3D ball. The excitement belongs in the movement, not the button.

FFlat gold, beside the barrecommended

A plain gold circle with a dark + and a soft shadow so it floats over the list. It's the one bright thing at the bottom of the screen, in the colour Chips already uses for what needs you. Because the button is ours, the window pours straight out of it.

Cost: a plain round view, so it's the cheapest of our own. The open question is placement. Either it sits over the slot iOS 26 keeps for a separate tab (a tab marked search), or in the gap beside the centred bar. I'd settle that with an hour on the simulator first, including what happens when the bar shrinks as the list scrolls.

TGold-tinted glass, beside the bar

The same circle in iOS 26's tinted glass: gold you can see the list through, matching the bar's material. Quieter than flat gold, and more of a piece with the bar.

Cost: the same as F, drawn with expo-glass-effect's GlassView and a gold tint, which is iOS's own glass. It's paler over light rows, so the + is a little less loud in light mode.

CClear glass, gold +

The system's own separate circle beside the bar, holding a +. Its glass, its press and its shrink-on-scroll are all iOS's.

Cost: the least code. It's a tab marked search and disabled, so it never opens, and we catch its press. The expo-router version here supports both. Two limits: the system's circle can't stretch, so the window grows from a copy laid over it, and the + may only come in grey, not gold. That second one needs checking.

BIn the bar, as a fourth tabnot recommended

A fourth item in the bar, New, with a gold +.

Cost: the same as C. It looks like a place to go rather than a thing to do, and it takes a quarter of the bar.

How the movement would be built

  • On the UI thread, with Reanimated. The window is one glass view laid over everything. Its content is laid out once at full size and faded in, so only one view's frame and corners change per frame and nothing reflows. That's ADR 0061's lesson: the room's own height is never animated.
  • Two springs, across and up, slightly out of step. That's what makes it pour instead of scale. The same pair runs in reverse when the window folds back into the +.
  • Into the room: the room opens under the window with no transition of its own. The window fills the screen, the first message flies from the field to its place, and then the window lifts away. The room already shows that message from the send queue, so there's no gap.
  • Haptics: a light tap on the + and a firmer one on send, from ui/haptics.ts.
  • Reduce Motion: the window fades in and cross-fades into the room. No stretch, no flight, no sweep.
  • Android: the same movement, with the + to the right of the bar. Not walked unless you ask.

Desktop and web

The + at the head of the sidebar, beside Recent, stays where it is. Today it opens a one-line form that makes a room nobody answers in. Instead it opens a small centred dialog with the same words and field, using a plain fade and scale of about 150 ms. Enter closes the dialog and opens the room the same way clicking a room does. No pouring, no flight.

What this changes in the plan

  • The connection mark stays top right. The + no longer competes for that corner, so nothing moves and the three placements from yesterday are dropped.
  • Phase 3 (phone) becomes: the + by the tab bar, the window, the move into the room, and the room's first words standing in as its name if the harness never names it.
  • Phase 4 (desktop) becomes the dialog above.
  • Phases 1, 2 and 5 are unchanged: the server, the SDK and test kit, and Jacobian's half.
