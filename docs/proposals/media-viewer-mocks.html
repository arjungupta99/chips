The media viewer, on every surface

What opens when a photo or a clip in a room is tapped, on the iPhone, an Android phone, the tablets and the desktop: one deck, one set of gestures, one stage — black, whatever the theme. The frames below are drawn at each device's own points and scaled; the gold markers are keyed to the notes under each figure. The photographs are gradients standing in for the golden scenario's: six from Priya's site visit, Arjun's receipt from the hardware store, and Scout's 43-second walkthrough.

The rules, in short

  1. One viewer. The same words, deck, arithmetic and rules on every surface — iPhone, Android, the tablets, the web; what differs is the hand, and what the platform owns: its back gesture, its share sheet, its bars.
  2. The deck is the media of the twenty messages around the tapped one, oldest first — the room's last twenty for a tap at the foot (Arjun, 13 September 2026); every photo and video those messages and route rows carry, a message's several standing together. Back is older, forward is newer; the ends resist.
  3. It opens from the tile and returns to it, drawn with the thumbnail already there; the room scrolls under the opaque stage so the return has a tile to land on.
  4. The first movement decides: sideways pages, down or up dismisses, a pinch zooms, a double tap zooms at the point, a tap hides the chrome.
  5. Dragging to dismiss shows the room; past 120 pt or a flick it goes, and the line is felt as a click.
  6. Three sizes, and the viewer climbs: thumbnail (on the device) → view copy (2,048 px) → original (on request).
  7. A video plays in the viewer: streamed on the server's network, downloaded with progress away from it; plays when landed on, pauses when left.
  8. Share, Save, Copy, Go to message, Details, Open original are one press away; on the desktop, Download, Copy image, Open message, Details, Fullscreen and a key for each. Save is a row because Android's share sheet has no Save Image.
  9. A screen reader gets the same viewer: modal, each item announced with its place, every action a button.
  10. An iPad — or an Android tablet — is the same viewer at the window's size: over the whole window, with a filmstrip and arrows where there is room.
  11. The platform's back closes it, with the return: the iPhone's edge swipe, Android's gesture or button, the Escape key.
