Proposal · the design system

The palette, lifted

Same hues, same character, more voltage. Chroma goes up on the coloured tokens, the inks pull apart from their grounds, and in dark the page drops away from the cards. Use the buttons at the top to dial it — everything on this page repaints. Lift is what I would ship.

What actually moves

  1. Chroma, not hue. Every coloured token keeps its hue angle in OKLCH and gains chroma — the status colours most (×1.22), the rest ×1.15, the brand gold least (×1.06, because it is the brand and should not wobble). Lightness is held except where contrast demands otherwise, so nothing changes its identity: blue is the same blue, louder.
  2. Ink pulls away from paper. Light --ink-2 and --ink-3 darken a step; dark --ink brightens a step. --ink-3 at 13px was 4.53:1 on --surface-2, a hair over the line; it lands at 4.9.
  3. In dark, the page drops. --bg goes #111215 → #0f1013 while the cards stay put, so a surface reads as raised rather than as the same grey. That is the whole Spotify trick — near-black page, cards a clear step above it, one loud accent.
  4. Two real failures get fixed on the way. Light --amber was 2.92:1 as a mark on white and --gray 2.71:1 — both under the 3:1 a non-text mark needs. They pass now.
  5. Nothing gets a new name. Same 29 tokens, same four blocks in tokens.css, same transcription on the phone. No component changes.

The eight avatar hues

Lifting the palette exposed something the muted version hid: the eight hues are really six. --avatar-1 and --avatar-7 sit 1.1° apart on the hue wheel — the same pink twice — and --avatar-4 has a chroma of 0.022, which is a grey with an opinion. At 44 points nobody noticed. At 56, next to seven saturated neighbours, they will.

What it costs

  1. One file is the change. packages/ui/src/styles/tokens.css — and it says the palette four times on purpose (:root, [data-theme=light], [data-theme=dark], and the media query), so it is four identical edits that tokens.test.ts already checks agree.
  2. Then the phone's transcription. apps/mobile/src/ui/tokens.ts holds the same values by hand; tokens.vitest.ts reads the stylesheet and fails on any drift, so it will tell me if I fat-finger one.
  3. Two tests move with it. sourceColours.vitest.ts pins avatarHue("Designer") to the literal #6c4fb5 — it becomes the lifted violet. Its contrast test is a real check and keeps passing: every hue clears 4.5:1 for the derived ground-and-ink pair in both themes, which I verified before writing this page.
  4. One value lives outside the system. OUTGOING on the phone — your own bubble's teal, #0d7c86 — is deliberately not a token. It lifts to #007c87 with everything else.
  5. No component touches it. Every colour in the packages is already var(--…); tokens.test.ts fails any stylesheet that names a hex. Nothing but the token files changes.
  6. Half an hour, plus the suite. The risk is not the edit, it is agreeing which stop. That is what the buttons at the top are for.

My call: Lift, with the respace. Lift on its own is the minor change you asked for and it is honest about it. The respace is the one extra thing I would push for, because two identical pinks is a bug you paid for twice and it gets worse the moment the faces grow. More is defensible if you want it to read younger; Vivid is there to show where it stops being Chips and starts being a slide deck.
