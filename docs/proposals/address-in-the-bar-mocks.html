Who it goes to, in the bar

Today the capsule floats as its own pill over the field, and its menu floats as a third piece of glass above that. Nothing about them says they belong to the bar. Each option below makes who-it-goes-to part of the bar itself, and the list, where there is one, grows out of the bar rather than appearing beside it. Each phone loops the same few seconds in a room with you, Kay and Lee: pick Kay, type, send. The keyboard is left out so the room stays readable; the bar stands on it in the app.

Now three pieces

The capsule, the +, the field and the menu are four separate pieces of glass. The menu springs out and swings past its size before it settles, which is the bounce you saw.
Cost: nothing. This is on your phone.

A · In the field recommended

The faces lead the field, and the field says who: "Message Everyone". The bar is one line again, and the room gets 42 points back. A tap on the faces opens the field itself upward into the list, so the list and the field are one piece of glass. A pick closes it back into the field. While you type, the faces stay at the front of the field. The desktop's field already says "Message …"; it would lead with the faces too.
Cost: phone ≈150 lines (the faces in the field, the field opening into the list on the room's own layout transition, the send's flight starting from the faces); the capsule and its menu go (−270). Desktop ≈90 for the same shape.

B · A tab on the field

The capsule becomes a tab joined to the field's top edge, one piece of glass with it, so the name stays visible above what you type. A tap grows the tab upward and wider into the list. On iOS 26 the join is UIKit's own: two pieces of glass that touch in one container flow together.
Cost: phone ≈120 lines. The join on the phone is UIKit's (GlassContainer), so the exact neck is its, not this drawing; the painted look below iOS 26 needs its own tab shape. Desktop ≈80.

C · A strip, no menu

Everyone and each agent sit in a strip on top of the field's glass, and one tap picks: there is no menu to open or close. It is always there in a room with two or more agents, and it scrolls sideways past three or four.
Cost: phone ≈110 lines; the capsule and menu go (−270). Desktop ≈70. It costs the room the same height as the capsule does today.
