From the field to the bubble

This is what happens between tapping Send and seeing your message in the room. Today the words vanish from the field, and the message fades in at the foot of the room as the room scrolls up to make space. Below are three ways to carry the words from the field into the bubble instead. All four phones type and send the same messages at the same moment, on a loop. Turn on slow motion to see each step.

The glass is the CSS stand-in from the last mock. The motion is not a stand-in: these are the springs and timings the app would run.

The same in all three

  • The field swells under your thumb as you press Send. That's the interactive glass already on your phone, not something new.
  • The time replaces sending… once the server has the message, as it does today.
  • Replies land as they do today. Only your own sends get the new motion.
  • The placeholder comes back once the words have left the field, not underneath them as they go. Today it shows the instant you tap.
  • With Reduce Motion on, all three do today's fade.
  • If you send from halfway up the history, the room still runs to the foot and the message lands the way it does today. The flight needs its landing spot on screen when it starts.
  • A photo sent from the staging chip lands the way it does today in the first version. The flight carries words. The chip could fly later if you want it to.

My call

A. It's what you described: the words you typed become the bubble, and your eye follows one thing from the field to the room. It's also what Messages does. B is the cheap version. It looks good through the glass, but the words disappear on the left while the bubble comes up on the right, so there's nothing to follow. C adds a lot of motion to something you'll do hundreds of times a day. I'd build A, live with it for a day, and add C's ripple only if A feels stiff.
