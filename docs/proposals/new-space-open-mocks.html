The + window: a way in without the yellow

Today the window pours out of the + in its gold, the sides a beat ahead of the top. On the phone, that reads as a wide yellow pill smeared along the foot of the screen before the glass shows (the first phone below). There are three other ways in, and they differ in how much of the + they keep.

These phones play by themselves, all at once: a tap on the +, the window up with the keyboard, then a tap outside to fold it away. Slow motion runs them at a fifth of the speed.

Pick one

Picked, 24 September 2026: NOW, the gold pour, kept ("for #2 I want the gold poured one"). On the phone it did not look like the mock, and not because of its design. The phone brings a keyboard up in a tenth of a second or so and draws nothing while it does, and a pour begun at the tap lost its first frames to that: two or three frames 50 to 60 ms apart, cold or warm. The pour now waits for the keyboard to start rising, or for 300 ms when no keyboard is coming, and runs from its first frame.

Later the same evening, on his phone: the window "oddly expands and contracts when it comes into position". The two springs were 250/24 and 185/20, which carried two per cent past the card, and past the card the window grows towards the whole screen, so it stood a line taller for a moment and shrank back. They are damped all the way now, 420/41 and 320/36, so they stop at the card, and stiffer, so they get there about as soon. NOW above runs the new ones.

The window pours out of the + in its gold, the sides a beat ahead of the top, and the gold fades as the glass grows.

AGlass, out of the +

The window still grows out of the + you tapped, but as glass from the first frame, with no gold. One spring moves the sides and the top together, so it swells instead of smearing. On the way, the + tips over into an × and travels to the window's corner, where it becomes the window's close button. The mark that opened the window is the one that closes it. Folding away runs the same path backwards.

Cost: small. In Window.tsx: drop the gold layer, drive both sides from one spring, and send the + glyph to the × instead of spinning it away. The clips, the send and the tests' shape stay as they are.

BUp with the keyboard

No growth. The window, whole, comes up from the bottom edge on the keyboard's own curve, as if it were the keyboard's top row. It is the calmest of the four and the closest to the system's feel. The link to the + goes: the window comes from the keyboard, not from what you tapped.

Cost: small. Replace the pour with a slide. The clips stay at the window's size, and the send's fill starts from there.

CFade in place

The desktop's move. The window fades in where it rests, scaling up a little, and rides the keyboard up. It has the least motion, and nothing ties it to the +.

Cost: smallest. A fade and a scale in place of the springs.
