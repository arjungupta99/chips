How far the blur reaches

Arrangement B everywhere here — back, name and mark in glass, the field one capsule with the + inside. The only thing that changes across the four is how far past the capsules the blur keeps going. Under the capsules it is always full strength; the number is the ramp below the top row and above the field, where the room is still meant to be readable.

Drag a frame to scroll it yourself.

What picking a number decides

  • Short ramps are ours to draw, not UIKit's. scrollEdgeEffects: 'soft' has one length and it is Apple's — roughly the fourth frame. Anything tighter means drawing the band in the app: a stack of five or six BlurViews, each masked by a gradient, which is the standard way to make a blur fade. Since B already brings expo-blur in for the capsules, that is one more package for the mask (@react-native-masked-view/masked-view) and no second rebuild.
  • And drawing it ourselves is what makes it match. With the native effect the top and bottom fades are whatever UIKit says; drawn, the two are the same number and the same curve, and they stay that way when the composer grows a second line or a staged-file capsule.
  • Under 16 it stops reading as a fade. At 12 the eye sees an edge again — not a hairline, but a place where the picture changes. The point of the ramp is that there is nowhere to point at.
  • The capsule does most of the work already. In B every capsule is real glass, so the words in it are legible against whatever is behind them without any band at all. The band is for the gap — the space beside the chevron, between the capsules, around the field — which is why it can be this short and still work.
  • The cost is per-frame blurring while the room scrolls. Five masked blur layers at each end is ten of them; on iOS that is GPU work the scroll already pays for, and it is the one thing I want on your phone before I believe it. Android gets a flat scrim instead.

My call

20 points, top and bottom. It clears the capsules by about half their height — enough that a bubble arriving under the field softens rather than clips — and it gives back nearly forty points of readable room against what you saw yesterday. 28 is the safe answer if you want the softness more obvious; I would not go past it, and I would not go under 16.
