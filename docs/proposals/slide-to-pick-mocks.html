Slide to Pick an Agent

Slide up from the faces to pick who a message goes to

Today you tap the faces at the front of the field, the list opens, and you tap a name. That is two taps, and the finger lifts in between. You asked for one movement: put the finger on the faces, slide up, and let go on the agent you want. Each phone below plays its version on a loop. You can press and slide in any of them yourself, with a mouse or on your phone.

Both options follow the same rules

  • A slide picks one agent alone, the same as a tap on a name. The circles stay for taps, for adding several.
  • Letting go where nothing is lit leaves who the message goes to as it was, and the list closes.
  • Each row the finger enters gives a light haptic tick. The mock can't vibrate, so the ring round the finger pulses instead.
  • In a room whose list scrolls (try 12 agents), hold the finger at the top of the list, or just above Everyone once you have been higher, and the list scrolls that way, faster the nearer the edge.
  • With the keys up, the list opens in the room the keys leave, as it does now.
  • VoiceOver keeps the button that opens the list. The slide is a shortcut on top of it.
